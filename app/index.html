    Road to the platinum

    About 200 hours, difficulty around 4/10. One playthrough is enough, every trophy is offline, and there is no mission replay — so save manually at the start and end of each chapter.

    1. 1. Play the story (60–80 hours)

      Finish all five chapters with Jason and Lucia. Story trophies unlock on their own — 12 chapter and mission trophies plus the gold for the final mission. Scan vehicles and study animals from the start: you will need 200 base-model vehicles and 100 animal species later.

    2. 2. Guard the one missable trophy

      After the mission Down to Earth, find Lucia at Jason's house in the morning and accept her invitation to meditate on the balcony. That meditation is gone after Chapter 1. Meditate at all 18 spots across Leonida (10 locations) for Positive Mental Attitude.

    3. 3. Reach 100% completion (about 100 hours)

      Open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion and work the checklist: 100 optional tasks, 39 Gang Operations, 22 Bank Jobs, 7 Payday Loan Jobs, 8 bank vaults, 15 of 18 card sets, 100 WAiNK scans, 33 underwater discoveries, 100 Cocaine Flamingos, 42 Shooting Range gold medals, 42 drone time trials, 300 stunts, 70 of 80 challenges, 15 of Bret's target decks. Earlier progress already counts.

    4. 4. Finish Strangers & Freaks and the leftovers (10–20 hours)

      Complete eight Stranger mission strands — Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone among them — then sweep up the remaining combat, driving, robbery and miscellaneous trophies. Check the list, and Vice Got a Price! unlocks with the last one.
